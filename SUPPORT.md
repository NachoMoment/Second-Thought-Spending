# Support without the shame spiral

Second Thought is a free-to-use independent project maintained by people, not a
staffed crisis service. We are happy to help with installation problems, reproducible
bugs, accessibility issues, and focused feature ideas.

## Where to ask

- **Bug or feature idea:** use the repository’s GitHub issue templates.
- **Private support email:**
  [secondthoughtproject.support@gmail.com](mailto:secondthoughtproject.support@gmail.com)
- **Security concern:** do not post sensitive details publicly. Use the support
  email and begin the subject with `SECURITY`.
- **Donations:** payment, refund, and receipt questions belong to Ko-fi or the connected
  donation provider. Do not include recovery details in a donation message.

## What support cannot provide

This project does not provide medical care, counseling, emergency monitoring,
or crisis response. In the United States, call 911 for immediate danger or a
possible overdose. Call or text 988 for crisis support. Outside the United
States, use the emergency and crisis resources where you are.

## Protect your privacy when reporting a bug

Please never include real risky-contact numbers, safe-contact numbers, names,
personal reasons, check-in notes, or screenshots containing them. Reproduce
the issue with clearly fake information whenever possible.

Useful technical details include:

- App version from **Menu → About & support**
- Phone model and Android version
- Whether gesture or three-button navigation is enabled
- The exact screen and safe reproduction steps
- Whether the app was installed through Android Studio, `phone:install`, or a
  downloaded GitHub Actions artifact
