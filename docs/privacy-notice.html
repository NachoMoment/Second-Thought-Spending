Second Thought Privacy Notice

Build: 1.0.0-community.3

Second Thought is free to use and has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, or remote recovery-data service.

Permanent privacy promise

Second Thought will not collect or store recovery data on a maintainer-owned server, whether the project is free, donation-supported, or proprietary. Information that you ask the app to remember is stored locally on your device so its features can work.

Information you choose to store

The app lets you store a nickname, recovery focus, personal reminder, reasons, safe contacts, risky contacts, check-ins, sobriety-timer information, notification choices, and privacy choices. This information stays locally on your device. In the Android app, these values are encrypted at rest using a key protected by Android Keystore.

Information sent to a maintainer

The app does not automatically transmit recovery information, diagnostics, or usage statistics. If you voluntarily email support or open a project issue, the maintainer receives only what you choose to send. Do not send real phone numbers, personal reasons, check-in notes, or other private recovery details.

Android permissions

If you enable risky-call protection, Android may grant Second Thought the call-redirection role. The app compares outgoing non-emergency numbers with the risky contacts saved on your device and can cancel the first matching attempt to show a pause. Android does not send emergency calls through this service.

If you enable reminders or the risky-call fallback, the app may request notification permission. Reminders are scheduled locally.

Calls, text messages, and outside services

When you choose Call or Text, Second Thought hands the number—and an optional drafted message—to the phone’s normal app. That app and the communications provider then handle the communication. Emergency and crisis links also open external phone or messaging services.

Optional donations

If you choose to open the donation page, you leave Second Thought and use an external donation and payment provider. Second Thought does not receive or store card, bank, or payout information. The provider may collect information needed to process the donation under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not include private recovery information in a donation message.

Retention and deletion

Information remains on the device until you edit it, remove it, use Privacy → Clear all app data, uninstall the app, or Android clears the app’s storage. There is no server copy to recover. Android application backup is disabled.

Security

Android builds use AES-GCM encryption with an app key protected by Android Keystore. No storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect access to the device, keep Android updated, and install APKs only from a source you trust.

Scope and safety

Second Thought is intended for adults. Crisis links in this build are designed for the United States. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It is not a substitute for professional care or emergency services.

Support

For private support, email secondthoughtproject.support@gmail.com. For non-sensitive bugs and feature ideas, use the project’s privacy-conscious GitHub issue templates once the repository URL is published. Never include real phone numbers, reasons, check-in notes, or other recovery details.
