Second Thought: Spending — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Second Thought: Spending is a free app for pausing purchases and tracking estimated money protected. It does not use accounts, ads, analytics, bank connections, or cloud synchronization.

Information stored on your device

When you enter items, the app stores their names, prices, categories, links, reasons, moods or triggers, reflection answers, decisions, and timestamps. It also stores your savings goals, selected protected apps, and settings. This information stays in the app's local storage on your device. The app does not send these entries to the developer or a server.

Optional shopping app protection

If you explicitly enable protection and Android Accessibility access, the service receives the package name of apps as they come to the foreground. It compares each name to the apps you selected. It does not save the names of apps you did not select. For a selected app, it displays a pause containing your local goal and estimated money protected. The service does not request access to screen content and does not read taps, messages, purchases, or passwords. App names and purchase entries are not transmitted or shared. You can turn protection off inside the app or in Android settings. Manual pauses remain available without this access.

Optional notifications

If you enable notifications and grant Android permission, cooldown reminders are scheduled on the device. No notification data is sent to our server.

Export, deletion, and retention

Entries remain on the device until you delete them through Settings → Delete all data and reset app, uninstall the app, or Android clears app data. Android application backup is disabled for this app. Export creates an unencrypted JSON file at a location you choose; anyone with access to that file can read it. You are responsible for copies you export or share.

Security

The app uses ordinary app-private device storage. It does not add its own encryption to purchase entries. Protect your device and exported files with a screen lock and current system updates.

Outside services

Opening an optional product link or sending a support email leaves the app. Those services receive the information you choose to send and apply their own privacy policies. The app itself has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Questions

Contact secondthoughtproject.support@gmail.com. Do not include purchase histories or other private data unless you choose to share them.
